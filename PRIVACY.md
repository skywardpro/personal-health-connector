# Privacy policy

Personal Health Connector is a tool for exporting your own health data. The developer runs no server and receives no data.

- **What is read:** only the data types you approve on Huawei's consent screen, from your own Huawei Health account, read-only.
- **Where it goes:** into a folder in your own Google Drive that you choose. Nowhere else.
- **What is stored on the phone:** access tokens issued to you by Huawei and Google, kept in the app's private storage, deleted when you sign out or uninstall.
- **What the developer sees:** nothing. There is no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting service in the app.
- **Sharing:** none. What you do with the exported files is up to you.
- **Deleting your data:** delete the exported folder in your Drive, revoke access in your Huawei and Google account settings, and uninstall the app.
