# User agreement

Personal Health Connector ("the app") is free, open-source software (MIT licence) that exports your own health data from Huawei Health to your own cloud drive.

1. **Your data, your account.** You may use the app only with your own Huawei ID and your own cloud drive. You must not use it to access anyone else's data.
2. **Consent.** The app reads only the data types you approve on Huawei's consent screen. You can withdraw consent at any time in your Huawei account settings and by uninstalling the app.
3. **No developer access.** The developer runs no server and receives none of your data. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
4. **Not medical advice.** The app only exports raw data. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and exported data may be incomplete or delayed.
